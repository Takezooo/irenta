iRenta is a comprehensive platform designed to simplify the management and search of rental properties. It enables property owners to manage listings, tenants, and payments seamlessly, while allowing seekers and tenants to find accommodations, book viewings, and manage their rental interactions in one place.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 👤 For Owners
- ➕ Add, ✏️ Edit, and 🏠 Manage property listings.
- 📋 Track tenant information and handle payment records.
- 🛠️ Receive and respond to maintenance requests.
- 📄 Create, edit, and send terms and lease agreements.

### 🙋‍♂️ For Tenants
- 🔍 Search for available properties.
- 📆 Request ocular visits and submit reservation requests.
- 💳 Track payment history and view pending payments.
- 🛠️ Submit maintenance requests with details.

### 🔍 For Seekers
- 🏘️ Browse and view property listings.
- 🔎 Search by location, price, or amenities.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Details

- **Frontend**: React.js
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express
- **Database**: MongoDB
- **Deployment**: Vercel (frontend) & Railway (backend)
